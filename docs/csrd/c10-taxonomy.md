# C10 EU Taxonomy KPIs

!!! warning "Criteria are changing, not legal advice"
    This page follows the Taxonomy Regulation and Delegated Regulation
    (EU) 2026/73, which applies from 1 January 2026. The Commission
    consulted in March and April 2026 on **revised technical screening
    criteria**, planned to apply from financial year 2027. Their adoption
    was not confirmed in an official source by 21 November 2026. Before
    your first report, check the consolidated text of Delegated Regulation
    (EU) 2021/2139 on EUR-Lex.

## In one sentence

Show what share of your turnover, capital expenditure (CapEx) and
operating expenditure (OpEx) comes from activities the EU Taxonomy
describes (eligible), and what share meets its criteria (aligned).

## Who does this and when

Finance (controlling), with the sustainability lead and the fleet,
property or production managers who hold the technical evidence. A
company that must publish sustainability reporting under the CSRD
also publishes these figures in the same statement (Article 8 of
Regulation (EU) 2020/852). See [C1](c1-scope.md) for the CSRD test.
Prepare them with the year-end accounts, because the denominators come
from the financial statements.

## Inputs

- The financial statements: net turnover, additions to fixed assets
  (CapEx) and the operating costs that count for OpEx. The definitions of
  the three KPIs are in Annex I of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2178.
- A list of your activities, with turnover, CapEx and OpEx for each.
- The activity descriptions and technical screening criteria in the
  Climate Delegated Act (EU) 2021/2139 and the Environmental Delegated Act
  (EU) 2023/2486, in their consolidated versions.
- Technical evidence for each activity you claim as aligned (for vehicles:
  type-approval data, tyre labels, recyclability data, a climate risk
  assessment).
- Your human rights and anti-corruption procedures, for the minimum
  safeguards ([C6](c6-topical.md)).

## Steps

### 1. Fix the three denominators

Take the turnover, CapEx and OpEx denominators from the accounts. Every
euro in a denominator will get exactly one status in step 6, so the
numerators can never add up to more than 100%.

### 2. Find your eligible activities

An activity is **eligible** if it matches an activity described in the
delegated acts, whether or not it meets the criteria. Match on what you
actually do, not on your NACE code: the codes in the delegated acts are
only hints. Activities that no delegated act describes are **not
eligible**.

### 3. Leave out non-material activities, within 10%

Since Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/73 you **need not assess**
activities whose turnover adds up to less than **10%** of the turnover
denominator. The same rule applies to CapEx. For OpEx:

- if OpEx is **not material to your business model**, you need not assess
  it at all, but you disclose the OpEx denominator and explain why;
- if OpEx is material, the same 10% rule applies.

Show the amounts you did not assess separately, as non-material.

### 4. Test each eligible activity for alignment

An activity is **aligned** when all four conditions of Article 3 of the
Taxonomy Regulation are met:

1. it **contributes substantially** to at least one of the six
   environmental objectives (Article 9), as defined by the technical
   screening criteria;
2. it does **no significant harm (DNSH)** to the other objectives, again
   under the technical screening criteria;
3. it is carried out with **minimum safeguards**: procedures in line with
   the OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises and the UN Guiding
   Principles on Business and Human Rights, including the ILO core
   conventions (Article 18);
4. it meets the **technical screening criteria** the Commission set in
   the delegated acts.

Keep the evidence for each condition: the assurance provider will ask for
it.

Some activities count as **transitional** (Article 10(2)): there is no
low-carbon alternative yet, and they meet the best-performance criteria
set for them. They count as aligned.

**Example: road freight (activity 6.6 of the Climate Delegated Act).**
In the original 2021 text it covers buying, leasing, renting and running
light and heavy goods vehicles (categories N1, N2, N3). Its substantial
contribution to climate change mitigation, in short:

| Vehicles | Substantial contribution when |
|---|---|
| N1 (light commercial) | Zero tailpipe CO2 |
| N2 and N3 up to 7.5 t | Zero-emission heavy-duty vehicles (Regulation (EU) 2019/1242) |
| N2 and N3 above 7.5 t | Zero-emission heavy-duty vehicles; or, where that is not feasible, low-emission heavy-duty vehicles (then transitional) |

The vehicles must not carry fossil fuels. The DNSH checks cover climate
risks (a climate risk assessment), circular economy (recyclable and
recoverable vehicles, waste managed in use and at end of life) and
pollution (tyres in the best noise and rolling-resistance classes in the
EU product database EPREL, the latest Euro VI step, and the vehicle noise
rules). Read the full criteria in the consolidated act, not this summary.

### 5. Do not double count

Allocate each euro once. If one asset serves several activities, split
its CapEx with a key you can explain (for trucks, for example, kilometres
or revenue per route). Revenue from an aligned truck is aligned; revenue
from the rest of the same fleet is eligible but not aligned.

### 6. Calculate and present the KPIs

For each KPI, give the share of the denominator that is **aligned**,
**eligible but not aligned**, **not assessed (non-material)** and **not
eligible**. Use the reporting templates in the annexes of Delegated
Regulation (EU) 2021/2178 as amended by 2026/73. The figures sit in the
sustainability statement, are covered by the assurance opinion
([C8](c8-assurance.md)) and are tagged with the rest of the statement
([C9](c9-tagging.md)).

## Worked example

Mediterranean Logistics Group (MLG) first reports for financial year 2027
([C1](c1-scope.md)). It does a **dry run on its 2026 figures**. It runs
150 heavy N3 trucks; 12 of them are battery-electric. All numbers are made
up.

**Eligibility and the 10% rule.** Road freight is activity 6.6. Selling
surplus electricity from its warehouse roofs matches activity 4.1 (solar
electricity), but it is only 0.3% of turnover, so MLG does not assess it.
Cold storage, handling and value-added services match no activity: not
eligible. The new cold store is CapEx under activity 7.1 (construction of
new buildings); MLG cannot yet show it meets the criteria, so it reports it
as eligible but not aligned.

**Alignment.** The 12 battery-electric trucks are zero-emission
heavy-duty vehicles and carry food, not fuel. MLG checked the DNSH
criteria (climate risk assessment for its depots, the manufacturer's
recyclability data, tyre classes in EPREL) and its minimum safeguards
procedures. Their share of road freight turnover comes from the revenue of
the routes they ran. The diesel trucks meet neither the zero-emission nor
the low-emission criteria: eligible, not aligned.

**Turnover** (denominator EUR 520,000,000):

| Item | Activity | Status | EUR | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Road freight with the 12 battery-electric N3 trucks | 6.6 | Aligned | 15,600,000 | 3.0% |
| Road freight with the diesel trucks (Euro VI) | 6.6 | Eligible, not aligned | 294,400,000 | 56.6% |
| Sale of surplus rooftop solar electricity | 4.1 | Not assessed (non-material) | 1,500,000 | 0.3% |
| Cold storage, handling and value-added services | - | Not eligible | 208,500,000 | 40.1% |

**CapEx** (denominator EUR 50,000,000):

| Item | Activity | Status | EUR | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 battery-electric N3 trucks | 6.6 | Aligned | 9,000,000 | 18.0% |
| Replacement diesel trucks (Euro VI) | 6.6 | Eligible, not aligned | 6,000,000 | 12.0% |
| New cold store (alignment not yet shown) | 7.1 | Eligible, not aligned | 30,000,000 | 60.0% |
| IT systems and racking | - | Not eligible | 5,000,000 | 10.0% |

**OpEx** (denominator EUR 18,000,000). Fleet maintenance is large for a
haulier, so OpEx is material and MLG does not use the OpEx exemption:

| Item | Activity | Status | EUR | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maintenance of the battery-electric trucks | 6.6 | Aligned | 900,000 | 5.0% |
| Maintenance of the diesel fleet | 6.6 | Eligible, not aligned | 13,500,000 | 75.0% |
| Maintenance of warehouse equipment | - | Not eligible | 3,600,000 | 20.0% |

**Summary of the dry run:**

| KPI | Aligned | Eligible (aligned + not aligned) |
|---|---|---|
| Turnover | 3.0% | 59.6% |
| CapEx | 18.0% | 90.0% |
| OpEx | 5.0% | 80.0% |

```python
import yaml
from esgcalc.csrd import taxonomy_kpi

tax = yaml.safe_load(open("data/examples/mediterranean_logistics.yml", encoding="utf-8"))["taxonomy_2026"]
for kpi in ("turnover", "capex", "opex"):
    k = taxonomy_kpi(tax[kpi]["rows"], tax[kpi]["denominator_eur"])
    print(kpi, f"{k.share('Aligned'):.1%}", f"{k.eligible_share:.1%}")
# turnover 3.0% 59.6% / capex 18.0% 90.0% / opex 5.0% 80.0%
```

`taxonomy_kpi` refuses rows that do not add up to the denominator, and
non-material amounts of 10% or more.

## Common mistakes

- **Confusing eligible with aligned.** Eligible only means the activity is
  described in a delegated act.
- **Using NACE codes as the test.** They are hints; the activity
  description decides.
- **Leaving out too much as non-material.** The activities you skip must
  add up to less than 10% of the denominator, and you show them separately.
- **Counting a euro twice**, for example an electric truck's CapEx under
  two activities.
- **Forgetting DNSH or minimum safeguards.** Substantial contribution
  alone is not alignment.
- **Using last year's criteria.** Check the consolidated delegated acts for
  each reporting year.

## Evidence to keep

- The reconciliation of each denominator to the financial statements.
- The activity list with the eligibility reasoning and the 10% calculation.
- For each aligned activity: the technical evidence for substantial
  contribution and each DNSH criterion, and the allocation keys used.
- Your minimum safeguards procedures and how you checked them.

File them under `csrd/taxonomy/` (see
[F1](../foundations/evidence-locker.md)).
