# C4 General disclosures

!!! warning "Not legal advice"
    This page summarises the structure of ESRS 2 in the handbook's own
    words. Read the standard itself for the full requirements.

## In one sentence

Every company reporting under the ESRS gives the general disclosures in
ESRS 2, whatever its material topics: how the statement was prepared,
governance, strategy and value chain, and how the materiality assessment
was done.

## Who does this and when

The company secretary, finance and the sustainability lead. Draft it during
the reporting year, and finish it after the materiality assessment and the
data are final.

## Inputs

- The materiality assessment ([C2](c2-dma.md)).
- Board and committee terms of reference.
- The business model and value chain description.
- Which phase-ins you will use.

## Steps

### 1. Know which version of the ESRS you use

The revised ESRS (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1563) are mandatory for
financial years starting on or after **1 January 2027**. They may be used
for financial years starting in 2026, if you say so.

### 2. Cover the four areas of ESRS 2

The revised ESRS are 12 standards: ESRS 1 (general requirements), ESRS 2
(general disclosures) and ten topical standards. ESRS 2 covers:

- **basis for preparation**, including any phase-in used;
- **governance**: who oversees sustainability matters, and how;
- **strategy**: business model, value chain, and financial effects;
- **management of impacts, risks and opportunities**, including a concise
  description of the materiality assessment, covering own operations and
  the value chain.

### 3. Decide which phase-ins you use

The revised ESRS let first-time reporters leave some disclosures out in
their first years. In the text this handbook read, companies first
reporting for financial years from 2027 may omit, for example:

- E4, S2, S3 and S4 disclosures (first two years);
- anticipated financial effects (first two years, except some E1
  paragraphs), and their quantitative part (first four years);
- comparative information (first year).

Say which phase-ins you use in the basis for preparation. These years were
read in EFRAG's text and **not yet checked against the Official Journal**.

### 4. Assign an owner and a date to each disclosure

Treat each disclosure as a small project with an owner and a deadline
before the statement goes to the auditor ([C8](c8-assurance.md)).

## Worked example

Mediterranean Logistics Group (fictional) plans its first ESRS 2
disclosures, for financial year 2027:

| Datapoint | What the group will write | Owner | Ready by |
|---|---|---|---|
| ESRS2-basis | Group statement for 2027; phase-ins used: anticipated financial effects (first two years) | Finance | 31 Jan 2028 |
| ESRS2-governance | Board audit committee oversees sustainability; operations director runs the programme | Company secretary | 30 Jun 2027 |
| ESRS2-strategy | Cold-chain distribution in Malta; suppliers include food producers and building-material importers; customers are supermarkets | Strategy | 30 Jun 2027 |
| ESRS2-materiality-process | The C2 assessment: who took part, the scales and thresholds, own operations and value chain | Sustainability lead | 31 Mar 2027 |

- The group does not need the E4, S2, S3 or S4 phase-ins: none of those
  topics was material in C2.
- The materiality process is ready first, because the topical disclosures
  depend on it.

## Common mistakes

- **Skipping ESRS 2 because a topic is not material.** ESRS 2 applies
  whatever the material topics.
- **A materiality description that only lists topics.** Explain the
  process, including the value chain.
- **Using a phase-in without saying so.** State it in the basis for
  preparation.
- **Mixing the old and revised ESRS.** Say which version you used.
