# C9 Digital tagging and publication

!!! warning "Not legal advice"
    Publication deadlines and filing rules come from national law. Tagging
    rules are not adopted yet. Check the current position before you
    publish.

## In one sentence

Publish the sustainability statement in its own section of the management
report, prepare the report in the European electronic format, and get
ready to tag it once the digital taxonomy and rules are adopted.

## Who does this and when

Finance and the company secretary, with the sustainability lead and the
auditor. Plan the timetable at the start of the reporting year.

## Inputs

- The final sustainability statement and the assurance opinion
  ([C8](c8-assurance.md)).
- Your national rules for approving, publishing and filing the annual
  report.
- The current status of the ESRS digital taxonomy.

## Steps

### 1. Put the statement in the management report

Sustainability reporting goes in a **dedicated, clearly identifiable
section of the management report** (for a group, the consolidated
management report). It is not a separate report.

### 2. Prepare the report in the electronic format

The management report is prepared in the **European single electronic
format** (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/815, "ESEF").

### 3. Get ready to tag

The sustainability reporting, including the EU Taxonomy disclosures, is
to be **marked up (tagged)** with a digital taxonomy. As of 20 November
2026 this is **not yet mandatory**:

- EFRAG published a draft XBRL taxonomy for the revised ESRS, with
  consultation until 11 November 2026, and plans to hand the final
  taxonomy over by the end of 2026;
- ESMA then drafts the tagging rules, and the Commission adopts them by
  amending Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/815.

Meanwhile, keep each reported figure linked to its datapoint id
([C7](c7-gap-analysis.md)). Mapping those ids to taxonomy elements later
is then quick.

### 4. Plan the timetable

Work backwards from your national publication deadline: drafting, the
assurance fieldwork, board approval, the assurance opinion, publication.

## Worked example

Mediterranean Logistics Group (fictional) plans its first report, for
financial year 2027. The dates are its own plan, not legal deadlines:

| Date | Step |
|---|---|
| 31 Jan 2028 | Sustainability statement drafted, all ESRS 2 and topical disclosures in |
| 28 Feb 2028 | Assurance provider's fieldwork finished |
| 20 Mar 2028 | Board approves the annual report, with the sustainability statement in its own section of the management report |
| 25 Mar 2028 | Limited assurance opinion signed |
| 15 Apr 2028 | Annual report published and filed under national law |

From statement draft to publication takes **75 days**. Because tagging
rules may be adopted before then, the group keeps a table of each figure
and its datapoint id, and checks the status of the tagging rules each
quarter.

## Common mistakes

- **Publishing a separate sustainability report.** It belongs in the
  management report.
- **Assuming tagging applies now, or never.** It is coming; check the
  status before each report.
- **Leaving the assurance opinion to the last week.** The provider needs
  time after the statement is final.
- **Forgetting national filing rules.** They set the deadlines.

## Evidence to keep

- The approved annual report and the signed assurance opinion.
- The figure-to-datapoint table.

File them in the evidence locker (see
[F1](../foundations/evidence-locker.md)).
