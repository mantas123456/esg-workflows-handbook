# Glossary

CBAM
:   Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism. An EU charge on the embedded emissions of certain imported goods.

CSRD
:   Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive.

DMA
:   Double materiality assessment: looking at a topic both for the company's impact on people and the environment, and for its financial effect on the company.

Embedded emissions
:   Greenhouse gases released while producing a good, including relevant precursors.

ESRS
:   European Sustainability Reporting Standards, the content rules for CSRD reports.

Scope 3
:   Indirect emissions in a company's value chain, upstream and downstream, outside Scopes 1 and 2.

VSME
:   The voluntary sustainability reporting standard for SMEs.
