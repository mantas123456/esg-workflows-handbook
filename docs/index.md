# ESG Workflows Handbook

Free, practical step-by-step workflows for the EU sustainability rules that
small and mid-sized companies actually meet.

| Track | Start with | For |
|---|---|---|
| **CBAM** | [CB1 Scope check](cbam/cb1-scope-check.md) | Importers of steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, hydrogen, electricity |
| **Scope 3** | [S2 Screening](scope3/s2-screening.md) | Anyone asked for value-chain emissions |
| **VSME** | [V1 Choosing the modules](vsme/v1-modules.md) | SMEs that get ESG questionnaires from customers or banks |
| **CSRD / ESRS** | [C1 Am I in scope?](csrd/c1-scope.md) | Companies still in scope after Omnibus I, and their advisers |

Not sure which applies? Start with [Which regime applies to me?](start-here/triage.md)

!!! warning "Not legal advice"
    The handbook explains the rules in its own words and links the sources.
    The regulation, the Commission's guidance and your competent authority
    always take priority. Every page shows when its facts were last checked.

See the [workflow map](workflow-map.md) for every step and the
[roadmap](roadmap.md) for what comes next.
