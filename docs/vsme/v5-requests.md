# V5 Answering customer and bank requests

!!! warning "Not legal advice"
    This page explains the value-chain cap in plain words and suggests
    wording for replies. It does not tell you whether a request is lawful.
    If a request is part of a contract or a legal duty, take advice.

## In one sentence

Answer ESG questionnaires from your VSME report, know which questions go
beyond the voluntary standard, and decide politely, case by case, whether
to share more.

## Who does this and when

Whoever handles customer and bank requests, usually sales or finance, with
the person who prepared the VSME report. Every time a questionnaire
arrives.

## Inputs

- The questionnaire and who sent it.
- Your VSME report ([V2](v2-basic.md), [V4](v4-comprehensive.md)).
- Your average number of employees in the previous financial year.
- Your contracts with the requester.

## The value-chain cap in plain words

- **Who it protects:** companies with on average **1,000 employees or
  fewer** in the previous financial year (headcount only).
- **Who it limits:** companies that report under the **CSRD**, when they
  collect information from their value chain for that report.
- **What it means:** such a company may not **require** you to give more
  than the voluntary standard covers. The limit is the list of datapoints in
  Annex II of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1560.
- **If they ask for more:** they must tell you **which** questions go beyond
  the cap, and that you have the **right to refuse** them.
- **Your size:** they may rely on your own statement of your employee
  numbers.
- **What it does not change:** you may still share more if you want to. It
  does not stop information commonly shared in your sector, and it does not
  override what a contract or a law already obliges you to give.
- **From when:** for their value-chain reporting from financial year 2027.

## Steps

### 1. Check who is asking, and why

Does the requester report under the CSRD, and is the request for that
report? Then the cap applies. A bank's loan conditions or a contract may
be a different matter.

### 2. Map each question to a datapoint

| Common question | VSME datapoint |
|---|---|
| How much energy do you use? | B3-energy |
| What are your Scope 1 and 2 emissions? | B3-scope1, B3-scope2-location |
| What are your Scope 3 emissions? | Not in the standard's Basic Module |
| Do you have reduction targets? | C3-targets |
| What climate risks do you face? | C4-climate-risks |
| How much waste do you produce and recycle? | B7-waste, B7-waste-recycled |
| How much water do you use? | B6-water-consumption |
| How many employees do you have? | B8-employees |
| How many work accidents? | B9-accidents, B9-fatalities |
| What is your gender pay gap? | B10-pay-gap |
| Any corruption convictions or fines? | B11-corruption |
| Do you have a human rights policy? | C6-human-rights-policies |
| What is the footprint of each product? | Not in the standard |

The datapoints are listed in [V1](v1-modules.md). Check Annex II of the act
for the exact list the cap refers to.

### 3. Answer from your VSME report

Give the same figures as in your report, with the same definitions. Send
the report itself where you can: it answers many questions at once.

### 4. Decide on questions beyond the standard

For each question the standard does not cover, choose: **share** (you
have the data and want to help), **share later** (you are working on it),
or **decline** (it would cost too much now). Declining is your right under
the cap; doing it politely keeps the relationship.

### 5. Keep a response bank

Save every answer with its date and source. The next questionnaire will ask
much the same.

## Reply wording you can adapt

**Sending the VSME report**

> Thank you for your questionnaire. We report under the voluntary
> sustainability reporting standard for smaller companies (Delegated
> Regulation (EU) 2026/1560). Our report for [year] is attached; it answers
> questions [numbers]. We are happy to explain any figure.

**Stating your size**

> For your information: we had on average [number] employees in [previous
> financial year].

**Asking which questions go beyond the cap**

> To help us answer, could you tell us which of these questions go beyond
> the voluntary standard? We understand that you are asked to say so.

**Sharing more, voluntarily**

> Question [number] goes beyond the voluntary standard. We have the data,
> and we are glad to share it. Please see the attached [inventory], with
> its methods and notes.

**Declining, politely**

> Question [number] goes beyond the voluntary standard, and we do not have
> this information at the moment. We would rather not estimate it roughly.
> We will let you know if this changes, and we are happy to discuss what
> would be most useful to you.

## Worked example

The supermarket that buys from Gozo Fresh Foods Ltd (fictional, 140
employees) reports under the CSRD and sends an eight-question supplier
questionnaire for 2026. Gozo is well under 1,000 employees, so the cap
protects it. All questions are made up.

| # | Question | VSME datapoint | Gozo's decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How much energy did you use in 2026? | B3-energy | Answer from the VSME report |
| 2 | What were your Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions? | B3-scope2-location | Answer from the VSME report |
| 3 | What were your Scope 3 emissions, by category? | Beyond the standard | Share voluntarily |
| 4 | Do you have a target to reduce emissions? | C3-targets | Answer from the VSME report |
| 5 | How much waste did you produce, and how much was recycled? | B7-waste | Answer from the VSME report |
| 6 | How many people work for you? | B8-employees | Answer from the VSME report |
| 7 | How many work accidents did you have? | B9-accidents | Answer from the VSME report |
| 8 | What is the carbon footprint per kg of each product you sell us? | Beyond the standard | Decline for now |

**6 of 8** questions are answered straight from the VSME report
([V2](v2-basic.md), [V4](v4-comprehensive.md)). **2** go beyond the
standard:

- **Scope 3 (question 3):** Gozo already has it ([S9](../scope3/s9-reporting.md))
  and shares it voluntarily: 5,780 ± 420 tCO2e, with the inventory notes.
- **Footprint per kg (question 8):** Gozo has no product-level figures and
  declines for now, using the polite wording above, and offers to discuss
  it for next year.

Gozo also asks the supermarket to confirm which questions it considers
beyond the cap, as the supermarket is required to say.

## Common mistakes

- **Answering everything with new estimates.** Use your VSME report; it is
  consistent and already checked.
- **Different figures for different customers.** Send the same numbers to
  everyone.
- **Refusing everything beyond the standard.** The cap gives you a right,
  not a duty to refuse. Share what you have if it helps.
- **Ignoring contracts.** The cap does not override what a contract or a
  law already requires.
- **No record of answers.** Keep a response bank.

## Evidence to keep

- Each questionnaire, your answers and the date sent.
- The statement of your size you gave.
- Any decision to decline, and why.

File them in the evidence locker (see
[F1](../foundations/evidence-locker.md)).
