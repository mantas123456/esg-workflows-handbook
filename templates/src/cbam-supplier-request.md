# Request for CBAM embedded emissions data for [year of production]

Dear [name],

We import [description of goods] made at your installation into the
European Union. Under the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM),
Regulation (EU) 2023/956, we must report the emissions embedded in these
goods each year and buy certificates for them.

We would like to use **your actual data** rather than the European
Commission's default values. Actual data can only be used when it has
been **verified by an accredited verifier**. Without it, we have to use
default values, which include a mark-up that rises each year.

## What we ask for

Please send us, for the calendar year **[year]** in which the goods were
produced:

| # | Information | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Installation name, location and identifier, and the CN codes produced for us | So we can match your data to our imports |
| 2 | Specific embedded emissions per CN code (tCO2e per tonne) | Direct emissions; indirect emissions too for cement and fertilisers |
| 3 | The relevant precursors used, with their mass consumed, origin and specific embedded emissions | For precursors with actual data: the verification report covering the period they were produced |
| 4 | The verification report from a verifier accredited for CBAM by a national accreditation body in the EU | Required before we can use actual data |
| 5 | Evidence of any carbon price effectively paid in your country for these emissions, net of any rebate or free allowances, with independent certification | Optional; may reduce the certificates we surrender |

## Please use the Commission's template

The European Commission publishes an **operator emissions report template**
for passing this data to EU importers. Please use it. You can find it,
with guidance for operators outside the EU, here:

- CBAM legislation and guidance:
  <https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/cbam-legislation-and-guidance_en>
- Guidance No. 2, Quick guide for non-EU operators:
  <https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2026-08/Guidance%20No.%202%20-%20Quick%20guide%20for%20non-EU%20operators%20on%20CBAM%20implementation.pdf>

You may also register your installation in the **Operators Portal of the
CBAM Registry**.
