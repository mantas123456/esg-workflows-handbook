# 3. Reply wording

**Sending the VSME report**

> Thank you for your questionnaire. We report under the voluntary
> sustainability reporting standard for smaller companies (Delegated
> Regulation (EU) 2026/1560). Our report for [year] is attached; it answers
> questions [numbers]. We are happy to explain any figure.

**Asking which questions go beyond the cap**

> To help us answer, could you tell us which of these questions go beyond
> the voluntary standard? We understand that you are asked to say so.

**Sharing more, voluntarily**

> Question [number] goes beyond the voluntary standard. We have the data,
> and we are glad to share it. Please see the attached [document], with its
> methods and notes.

**Declining, politely**

> Question [number] goes beyond the voluntary standard, and we do not have
> this information at the moment. We would rather not estimate it roughly.
> We will let you know if this changes, and we are happy to discuss what
> would be most useful to you.
