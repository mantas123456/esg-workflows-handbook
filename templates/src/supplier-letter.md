# Request for emissions data for [year]

Dear [name],

We are preparing our greenhouse gas inventory for [year], including the
emissions of the goods and services we buy (Scope 3). You are one of our
main suppliers. We would like to use **your own figures** rather than an
industry average, because they describe your products better.

## What we ask for

For the calendar year **[year]**:

| # | Information | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your greenhouse gas emissions (tonnes CO2e) for the products we buy, cradle to gate: Scope 1, Scope 2 and the upstream part (what you buy) | If you only have Scope 1 and Scope 2, send those and tell us |
| 2 | Your total output in **[tonnes / units / EUR revenue]** | The same unit as our purchases, so we can work out our share |
| 3 | What the figure covers: sites, gases, cradle to gate or not | One or two sentences is enough |
| 4 | Has the figure been verified? By whom? | Unverified figures are welcome too |

We bought **[quantity and unit]** from you in [year], according to our
records. Please tell us if your records differ.

If you have not calculated your emissions, your **energy bills** (electricity
in kWh, fuels in litres or kWh) and your **production volume** for the year
are enough: we can do the calculation and share it with you.

## Timing

Please reply by **[date]**. Our customer needs our inventory by [date].

## How we will use it

We will use your figures only for our greenhouse gas reporting to
[customers / lenders / our report], and we will tell you how they were
used.

Thank you for your help.

Kind regards,

[Name]
[Position]
[Company]
